ABOUT ME

Stories, Shaped by Legacy

As the Managing Director of Layam, my journey in visual storytelling is deeply inspired by my father — the person who first showed me the value of dedication, creativity, and meaningful work.

Watching his journey and the passion he brought to every project became one of my greatest inspirations. His work taught me that true craftsmanship is not simply about what we create, but about the emotion, purpose, and legacy we leave behind.

Today, I carry that inspiration into Layam, building on those values while creating a vision of my own. From intimate celebrations and personal milestones to brands, people, and stories that deserve to be remembered, our approach remains rooted in authenticity, emotion, and cinematic artistry.

For me, filmmaking is not just about capturing what happens in front of the camera. It is about noticing the moments between the moments — the emotions, connections, details, and stories that might otherwise disappear with time.

My vision for Layam is to create films that transcend the moment they were made — films that can be revisited years later and still bring back the same feeling.

“Inspired by where it began. Driven by where it can go. Creating stories that last beyond the frame.”
